# ARV Trademark and Identity Policy

## Purpose

The open-source licenses in this repository govern copyright permissions. They do not grant a right to create confusion about origin, affiliation, endorsement, certification, or official ARV status.

No statement in this repository asserts that any ARV identifier is a registered trademark.

## Project identifiers

Current ARV project identifiers include:

- ARV;
- ARV Snap;
- ARV Proof OSS;
- ProofOps.

## Accurate references

Accurate nominative references such as `fork of ARV Proof OSS`, `based on ARV Proof OSS`, or a precise compatibility statement are acceptable when they are truthful and non-confusing.

An independent implementation should use its own distinct primary product, service, issuer, and visual identity.

## Official status

Compatible source code, schema compatibility, copied identifiers, or independently generated cryptographic signatures do not establish official ARV authorization.

Official-status claims require a separate applicable governance and trust determination.
