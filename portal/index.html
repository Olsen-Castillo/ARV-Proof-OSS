What this page does not establish

A matching artifact digest establishes only that the selected artifact bytes match the SHA-256 digest disclosed in the loaded manifest. This page does not verify the manifest Ed25519 signature, the full-envelope commitment, trust authorization, official ARV status, completeness of evidence, or material truth.
